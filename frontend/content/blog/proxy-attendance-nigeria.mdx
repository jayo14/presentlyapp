## A quiet problem that became normal

Proxy attendance is no longer an edge case in many Nigerian universities. It is now a social system. One student signs for another. A screenshot of a check-in circulates. A friend marks attendance for five people in ten minutes.

What makes this dangerous is not only the dishonesty. It is that the institution starts making academic decisions on incorrect data.

## Why proxy attendance persists

### 1. Manual systems are easy to game

Paper registers and verbal roll calls are not evidence. They are convenience tools. If attendance contributes to grading, students will always optimize the loophole.

### 2. Large classes and weak verification

A lecturer handling 200 students cannot manually validate who is physically present each session. Administrative pressure and time constraints make shortcuts attractive.

### 3. No trustworthy source of truth

When attendance data can be altered, backfilled, or submitted remotely without constraints, no one can confidently defend it at exam clearance or disciplinary review.

## The real cost to universities

- Students who attend consistently are penalized by the same score as students who rarely show up.
- Lecturers lose confidence in compliance analytics.
- Departments cannot trust attendance trends for early intervention.
- Institutions face integrity risk when disputes escalate.

Attendance should not be ceremonial. It should be actionable.

## What a modern attendance system must do

A practical system for Nigerian campuses should be:

- **Device-native:** works on the smartphone students already carry.
- **Low-friction:** no expensive biometric hardware at every classroom.
- **Location-aware:** validates presence in the approved venue boundary.
- **Auditable:** every check-in should be traceable and explainable.

## How Presently addresses the problem

Presently combines GPS verification and session constraints so that students can only check in when they are physically within a lecturer-defined location radius.

Core protections include:

- Venue-aware check-in validation.
- Session windows with strict timing rules.
- Centralized logs for lecturer and admin review.
- Real-time dashboards for compliance monitoring.

## Why this matters now

Nigerian universities are scaling digital infrastructure quickly. Attendance should not remain one of the last unresolved integrity gaps.

If institutions want better performance insight, fair grading support, and transparent exam clearance workflows, attendance data quality has to improve first.

## Final thought

Proxy attendance is not a student culture issue alone. It is a systems design issue. When systems reward weak verification, abuse is predictable.

Fixing attendance is not about policing students. It is about building trust in academic records.
