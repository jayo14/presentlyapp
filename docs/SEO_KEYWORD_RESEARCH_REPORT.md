## 1. Nigerian Higher Education Search Landscape

Search behavior across Nigerian tertiary education is characterized by distinct user personas operating with varying degrees of digital maturity:

1. **Lecturers:** Search pragmatically on mobile and desktop when seeking tools to manage heavy teaching workloads, calculate grading continuous assessments (CA), or curb student cheating. Queries tend to be problem-focused and workflow-oriented.
2. **Heads of Department (HODs) & Deans:** Search during departmental reviews, pre-accreditation audits, or end-of-semester exam clearance crises. Queries emphasize "management", "system", "reporting", and "clearance".
3. **ICT Directors & University Registrars:** Search with formal procurement intent. They evaluate institutional software, integration with university portals, and scalability across faculties.
4. **Students & Course Reps:** Search casually to verify how attendance is tracked, whether GPS attendance can be bypassed, or how to check semester attendance percentages.

> **Data Integrity Notice:** In accordance with project brief constraints, search volume and keyword difficulty metrics are based on regional Google Search data indicators for Nigeria where available. Where localized volume data is sparse or unsegmented by third-party tools, metrics are explicitly labeled as **[Niche / Low Vol]** or **[Metric Unavailable]** rather than manufacturing synthetic figures.

## 3. SERP Intent & Feature Opportunities

1. **Featured Snippets (Position Zero):**
   - Query: *"How does GPS attendance verification work?"*
   - Strategy: Use a clean 3-step numbered list format (`<ol>`) on `/how-it-works` and `/blog/how-presently-works` with exact character counts (40–55 words) optimal for Google’s snippet extraction.
2. **People Also Ask (PAA):**
   - PAA queries in Nigeria frequently center on: *"Can GPS be spoofed for attendance?"*, *"What is the minimum attendance percentage for Nigerian universities?"*, *"Can students check in without internet?"*
   - Strategy: Directly answer these queries using structured H3 FAQ questions with accordion schema on `/` and `/faq`.
3. **Local Knowledge Panel & Sitelinks:**
   - Deploy `Organization` and `WebSite` JSON-LD schema with exact brand identifiers to trigger Google Knowledge Graph recognition for queries regarding "Presently".
