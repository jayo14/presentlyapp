## 1. Editorial Strategy & Philosophy

- **Zero Generic AI Fluff:** Every article is written from the perspective of an academic peer who understands the daily friction of 250-seat amphitheaters, spotty 3G data, generator changeovers, and semester examination clearance dockets.
- **Actionable & Practical:** Articles provide real calculators, checklists, and templates that lecturers and department heads can use immediately.
- **Topical Clustering:** Articles interlink tightly with Presently’s core product and pricing pages, transferring search equity directly to high-converting commercial endpoints.
