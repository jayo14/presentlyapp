# PRESENTLY VISUAL BIBLE: "PRESENTLY REALISM"
**Project**: Presently — Trust Motion Launch Film  
**Version**: 1.0 (Locked Style Specification)  
**Target Output**: Premium Documentary Realism for Nigerian Higher Education Technology

---

## 1. Core Visual Philosophy
Presently Realism is a restrained, premium documentary and editorial aesthetic designed for a serious Nigerian higher-education technology platform. It avoids startup tropes, stock-photo clichés, and AI artifice in favor of grounded, physical reality punctuated by precise institutional technology.

- **Feels like**: Modern institutional infrastructure, editorial photojournalism, authentic university life, quiet confidence, precision software.
- **Never feels like**: Cartoonish, synthetic, cyberpunk, generic SaaS, stock photography, AI-generated surrealism, or hyperbolic commercial advertising.

---

## 2. Subjects & Human Casting
- **Primary Subjects**: Authentic contemporary Nigerian university students and lecturers.
- **Demeanor & Posture**: Natural, focused, candid. Students moving between lectures, checking phones in transit, entering amphitheaters, consulting schedules.
- **Physical Characteristics**: Authentic Nigerian skin tones, natural skin texture with realistic pores and lighting falloff; natural hair textures (fade cuts, braids, locs, natural afros, simple head coverings); anatomically correct hands, fingers, and eyes.
- **Expressions**: Neutral to engaged, authentic concentration, subtle relief upon verification. Zero forced stock-photography smiles; zero direct staring into camera lenses unless intentionally framing an editorial portrait.
- **Wardrobe**: Contemporary, casual collegiate attire (hoodies, button-down shirts, simple t-shirts, backpacks, minimal watches). No exaggerated traditional costumes unless contextually appropriate for faculty ceremonial events; no generic corporate uniforms.

---

## 3. Nigerian Context & Environment
- **Campus Architecture**: Modern Nigerian federal and private university campuses (brutalist/modernist concrete lecture halls, glass-and-steel faculty pavilions, shaded covered walkways, tiered amphitheaters, lecture desks with wooden armrests, campus foliage).
- **Authenticity Standard**: Believable, dignified, real-world educational infrastructure.
- **Strict Prohibitions**:
  - NO poverty-porn or exaggerated rustic depictions.
  - NO exaggerated safari or tropical clichés.
  - NO generic Western collegiate gothic architecture (ivy-covered brick red-brick Oxford/Harvard tropes).
  - NO arbitrary cultural props inserted merely to "signal" Africa.

---

## 4. Camera & Cinematography
- **Lens Perspective**: 35mm and 50mm documentary focal lengths. Natural perspective with realistic human field of view.
- **Framing**:
  - Environmental wide shots establishing architectural scale and institutional credibility.
  - Medium shots following students in transit.
  - Controlled close-ups on hands interacting with smartphones.
- **Depth of Field**: Controlled, realistic optical depth of field (f/2.0 to f/4.0). Gentle background bokeh that preserves environmental context without turning backgrounds into unreadable blur.
- **Movement**: Subtle dolly pushes, tripod stability, controlled handheld tracking with human breathing weight. No impossible drone acrobatics, no extreme fisheye, no CGI camera spirals.

---

## 5. Lighting & Atmosphere
- **Primary Source**: Natural equatorial daylight, soft overcast diffusion, or clean morning sunlight.
- **Interior Lighting**: Realistic architectural fluorescent or LED classroom panels mixed with natural window ambient light.
- **Contrast & Black Levels**: Deep, rich blacks; controlled shadow roll-off; crisp highlights without digital blowout.
- **Brand Glow**: Do NOT wash entire scenes in yellow or gold light. Environment lighting remains photoreal; Presently Gold is reserved for UI overlays, verification pulses, and subtle glass reflections.

---

## 6. Color Palette & Grading
- **Environment Palette**: Neutral concrete greys, obsidian darks, subtle earthy campus greens, warm natural wood tones.
- **Brand Accents**:
  - **Presently Gold**: `#F4C542` / `#F9C406` (Primary accent, brand horizon, active scanning states).
  - **Verification Green**: `#10B981` / `#22C55E` (Successful biometric and location check-in state).
  - **Presently Black / Obsidian**: `#0B0B0C` to `#161512` (Premium dark foundation).
  - **Typography Neutral**: Pure White `#FFFFFF` for primary titles; Muted Gray `#9CA3AF` / `#6B7280` for secondary metadata.
- **Banned Palettes**: No cyan/magenta cyberpunk neon, no purple SaaS gradients, no harsh orange-and-teal LUT abuse.

---

## 7. Realism Quality Bar (Zero-Tolerance Checklist)
Every candidate asset must pass the following criteria before approval:
1. **Hands & Anatomy**: Exactly 5 fingers per hand, natural knuckle creases, realistic nail beds, natural phone grip.
2. **Devices**: Believable modern smartphone proportions (sleek bezel, flat glass, accurate camera cutout); no warped screen borders.
3. **Facial Geometry**: Symmetrical gaze, natural pupil reflection, realistic ear placement, natural lips.
4. **Lighting Directionality**: Consistent shadow vectors between subject, device, and surrounding environment.
5. **Surface Textures**: Realistic fabric weaves, matte phone cases, textured concrete, no plastic AI sheen.

Any asset exhibiting anatomical distortion, synthetic gloss, or hallucinated geometry must be logged into `rejection-log.md` and re-generated immediately.
